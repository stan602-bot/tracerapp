GETTING STARTED

Trace right over your ghost 👻

1. Tap Camera and allow access. Point your phone at the ghost while it rests in a stable position.

2. Choose Back cloak or Front cloak. Drag the yellow ghosts into place. Pinch to resize, or use the sliders below.

3. Set transparency, then tap Lock position so you don't accidentally move the pattern while you paint.

4. To compare your work, tap Hide pattern. Use Save image to capture the tracing view.

Important: This app does not track the ornament as you move your phone. Keep the phone on a stand for best results. Camera access requires HTTPS or localhost.
